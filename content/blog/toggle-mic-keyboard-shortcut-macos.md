If you recently switched from Windows to macOS, you may have noticed that muting and unmuting the microphone is not as quick by default. I wanted a one-key shortcut that works anywhere, and this setup solved it.

This guide walks you through the exact steps to create a global mic toggle on your Mac.

## What You Need

- A Mac running a recent version of macOS
- The Toggle Mic shortcut
- Two minutes of setup time

## Step 1: Download the Shortcut

Download and add the shortcut first:
[Download Toggle Mic Shortcut](https://www.icloud.com/shortcuts/b04fc87377d94fa0bce3ce7087843cf7)

After adding it, confirm the shortcut appears in your Shortcuts app.

![Step 1: Toggle Mic shortcut visible in Shortcuts app](/img/blog/toggle-mic/step-1-shortcut-visible.png)

## Step 2: Move It to Quick Actions

Open the Shortcuts app, find the shortcut, and enable it for Quick Actions.

![Step 2: Move shortcut to Quick Actions](/img/blog/toggle-mic/step-2-move-to-quick-actions.png)

## Step 3: Open Keyboard Shortcut Settings

Go to:
System Settings -> Keyboard -> Keyboard Shortcuts

This is where you can assign a key combination to your Quick Action.

![Step 3: Keyboard settings page with Keyboard Shortcuts highlighted](/img/blog/toggle-mic/step-3-keyboard-settings-marked.png)

## Step 4: Find Toggle Mic Under Services

Open Services, then expand the Shortcuts section until you find Toggle Mic.

Double-click it and set your preferred keybind. I use Fn + F5 because that key already has a mic icon.

![Step 4: Services page with Toggle Mic highlighted](/img/blog/toggle-mic/step-4-services-toggle-mic-marked.png)

## Step 5: Test the Shortcut

After saving, run the shortcut and check the menu bar for the mic status indicator.

![Step 5: Toggle Mic shortcut running with menu bar indicator](/img/blog/toggle-mic/step-5-menu-bar-running.png)

## Result

You now have a fast, system-wide way to mute and unmute your microphone without opening any app menus.
